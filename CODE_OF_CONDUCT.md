# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a straightforward, low-drama place to contribute. Most
contributions here are small factual corrections, and the people making them are
often founders doing it between other work. Everyone should be able to take part
regardless of experience, background, identity, or how technical they are.

## Expected behaviour

- **Assume good faith.** A wrong entry is almost always a mistake, not carelessness.
- **Be specific and kind in review.** "This says the vendor requires $500k minimum,
  but the page says maximum" is useful. "This is wrong" is not.
- **Welcome non-technical contributors.** Someone reporting a rejection they just
  received is giving this project its most valuable data. They should never be made
  to feel they submitted it incorrectly.
- **Disagree about facts, not people.** When two readings of a vendor page conflict,
  the answer is to quote the page — not to escalate.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind
- Discriminatory language or imagery
- Publishing others' private information
- Submitting affiliate links, paid placements, or entries with an undisclosed
  commercial interest, then arguing about it when it is removed
- Deliberately submitting unverified or fabricated data

That last one matters more here than in most projects. The entire value of this
list is that its claims are traceable. Knowingly adding a figure that is not on the
vendor's page damages every other contributor's work.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space where someone is
representing the project.

## Reporting

Report concerns to **tayyabqa4@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately. We will
acknowledge receipt, look into it, and respond.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this in good faith may be removed from the
project.
